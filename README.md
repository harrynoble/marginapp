# Margin

An adaptive personal day planner for Android. Not a to-do list: tasks and commitments
are inputs, and the day's schedule is the output. Margin knows your timetable, your
deadlines, your build time and your leisure floor, and it answers one question all day:

**"Given everything I know about your day, what should you do now?"**

- Native Android, Kotlin + Jetpack Compose + Material 3
- Offline-first: the planner, timetable, tasks, notifications and history all work with no network
- AI is an enhancement layer for natural language, never a dependency
- A deterministic, unit-tested scheduling engine with no Android dependencies
